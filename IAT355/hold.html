...So what about Opera?
Was Timothee Chalamet right?

Opera's limited presence on Broadway relative to other show types stems from two structural barriers. First, the genre carries formal audience expectations rooted in classical convention, demanding a level of cultural familiarity that musical theatre does not require.

Second, many canonical operas are performed in Italian, German, or French, limiting accessibility for the predominantly English-speaking Broadway audience. Together, these factors have historically kept opera from competing for mainstream Broadway programming, confining it to a small and consistent share of productions across the dataset's two-century span.
